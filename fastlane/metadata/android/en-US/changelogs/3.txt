What's new in v0.1.2:

- Sleep stage classification is now smoother and more accurate: a rolling window evens out stage flicker, and the final moments of a session are always counted
- Fixed a crash when tapping habit rows (caffeine, alcohol, stress) in Daily Log
- The morning/evening chronotype quiz now uses the real rMEQ scale (4-25), and older results are corrected automatically
- Habit insights now show how confident a correlation is and how many nights it is based on
- Onboarding life-stage options refined, with an explicit "prefer not to say" choice; recommended sleep-hours display fixed
- Alarms: the in-app firing screen is reachable again and each alarm's wake-confirmation window is honored
- Fixed a race where the morning deep-sleep alert could be dropped when a tracking session ended
- Fixed the stop button becoming unreachable if you pressed back during sleep tracking: back is now locked while a session is running, and the Home screen always returns you to the stop screen
- Fixed the morning review occasionally showing a previous night's session after the app was restarted mid-tracking
- Tapping the ongoing sleep-tracking notification now opens the tracking screen, so you can stop a session from anywhere
